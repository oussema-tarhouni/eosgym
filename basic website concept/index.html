About Us

A gym built by people who actually train here

EOS started as a single strength floor and a stubborn belief that a gym should feel like it belongs to its members, not the other way around. A decade later that hasn't changed — the same coaches who designed the programming are still on the floor spotting sets and correcting form.

We built three things under one roof: a serious free-weight and machine floor, a reformer pilates studio led by instructors who watch every rep, and a class schedule people actually show up for. No matter which door you walk through, someone knows your name and your goals.
