FAQ
How do I cancel a booking?⌄

Cancel up to 2 hours before class starts from Schedule → your booking, or from the Booking History page.

How do I freeze or cancel my membership?⌄

Membership freezes and cancellations are handled at the front desk or via WhatsApp/email above.

I lost my membership card — what now?⌄

Your app is your digital membership card — just show your Profile at check-in, no reissue needed.

Help & Support
TERMS OF SERVICE
Last updated: August 2026

1. Membership

Your membership grants access to EOS Fitness & Pilates according to the plan you choose (Basic, Gold, or Elite). Access hours, class allowances, and perks follow the plan details shown on the Plans page at the time of purchase or renewal.

2. Bookings & Cancellations

Classes can be booked through the app subject to available spots. Cancel at least 2 hours before class starts to free your spot for other members. Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may affect your future booking priority.

3. Billing & Renewal

Memberships renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled beforehand. You can review your renewal date anytime from the Payment Methods page.

4. Studio Conduct

Members are expected to follow instructor guidance, respect equipment and studio spaces, and treat staff and other members courteously. EOS Fitness & Pilates reserves the right to suspend access for conduct that endangers others.

5. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms from time to time to reflect changes to our services. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised terms.

Help & Support
PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: August 2026

1. What We Collect

Your name, email, phone number, and the emergency contact you choose to provide, along with your class bookings, attendance, and membership plan.

2. How We Use It

To run your account, manage bookings and check-ins, send reminders and renewal notices, and keep your membership status up to date. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Payment Information

Payments made through D17, Flouci, Visa, or Mastercard are processed by those providers directly — EOS Fitness & Pilates does not store your card numbers or wallet credentials in the app.

4. Your Choices

You can update your personal info anytime from Edit Profile, adjust what you're notified about from Notifications, and request account deletion by contacting us.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account and booking history for as long as your membership is active, plus a limited period afterward for billing and legal record-keeping.

Help & Support
COOKIE POLICY
Last updated: August 2026

1. Essential Cookies

Used to keep you logged in, remember your session, and make the app function correctly. These can't be turned off without affecting core functionality.

2. Preference Cookies

Remember small choices like your last-viewed page or selected filters, so the app feels consistent between visits.

3. Analytics Cookies

Help us understand which features are used most, so we can improve the booking flow and class scheduling. This data is aggregated and not shared for advertising purposes.

4. Managing Cookies

You can opt out of analytics cookies anytime by contacting us at support@eosfitness.tn or through the front desk.
